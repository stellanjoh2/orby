About

For over twenty years I've worked where games, 3D and brand meet. Shipping titles at studios, running agencies, shaping how platforms look for millions of creators. That's been the through-line.

I'm Stellan Johansson, a Swedish creative director and brand designer. I came up through environment art, motion, UI and visual identity. My first job was 3D artist at Splash Damage on Enemy Territory: Quake Wars. After that I did art and UI at GRIN on Bionic Commando, Wanted: Weapons of Fate and Terminator: Salvation, then joined People Can Fly as UI Artist & Designer on Bulletstorm.

I co-founded two agencies later on: 1910 Design & Communication and Ritual Digital Agency. We built brands and digital experiences for gaming, automotive, PC hardware and entertainment clients. Big website builds for the likes of Fractal Design, plus game credits on The Ascent, A Way Out and others.

Most recently I was Creative Director for Creator Marketplaces at Epic Games, leading brand for Quixel, Sketchfab and Fab. I also worked as Principal Brand Designer across the Unreal Engine ecosystem and Fortnite.

Orby started as me trying to understand code and 3D on the web. What it feels like to build something alone from scratch. I'm not a developer, and I'd rather say that upfront before anyone opens the repo and wonders what I was thinking. It was a learning project with Three.js and a lot of AI help. Real developers will probably find plenty to cringe at. That's okay. Perfect code was never the goal. I just wanted to see how far curiosity could go. Turns out, pretty far.

So here it is. Hope it's useful.

If you're in games or 3D and need a creative director or brand designer who gets that world, I'd love to hear from you.
