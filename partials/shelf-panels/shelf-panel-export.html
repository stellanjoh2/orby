Export

Image

Raster snapshot of the current view—lighting, materials, and post effects included. Choose PNG, JPEG, or WebP; toggle transparency when the format supports it. Size is relative to the viewer—1× matches your current screen resolution, 2× exports at double that (larger file, more detail).

SVG

Vector output derived from the current framing—great for logos, precision edges, and scaling without pixels. Silhouette traces a single outline; Color builds flat filled regions from the render.

GLB

Export a .GLB for other 3D tools and AR — from an imported SVG extrusion, or from Shader Lab Voxel HD, PS2 Crush, PSX, or VGA/DOS 3D (with the look applied). Tip: dial in extrusion or Shader Lab before exporting.

Video

Combine object turntable, camera orbit/dolly/roll, and optional HDRI rotation. Full and subtle spins apply only when Turntable or Orbit is enabled — they rotate the mesh or camera, not the environment.

MP4 is a real-time viewport capture, not an offline render. Heavier scenes may drop frames or fail—best for previews and lighter setups. For dependable output, export a PNG sequence.
