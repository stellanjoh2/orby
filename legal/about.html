About

I've spent over 20 years at the intersection of games, 3D and brand — from shipping titles at world-class studios to building agencies, to defining the visual identity of platforms used by millions of creators worldwide.

I'm Stellan Johansson, a Swedish creative director and brand designer with roots in environment art, motion, UI and visual identity. I started my career as a 3D artist at Splash Damage on Enemy Territory: Quake Wars, then moved into art and UI roles at GRIN — working on Bionic Commando, Wanted: Weapons of Fate and Terminator: Salvation — before joining People Can Fly as a UI Artist & Designer on Bulletstorm.

I later founded two agencies, 1910 Design & Communication and Ritual Digital Agency, where I built brands and digital experiences for clients across gaming, automotive, PC hardware and entertainment — including large-scale website projects for companies like Fractal Design — with game credits including The Ascent, A Way Out and many more.

Most recently I served as Creative Director for Creator Marketplaces at Epic Games, leading brand across Quixel, Sketchfab and Fab — three of the most significant asset marketplaces in the 3D and games industry — and as Principal Brand Designer across the Unreal Engine ecosystem and Fortnite.

Orby started as a personal learning experiment — a way to understand code and 3D on the web with Three.js, and what it actually feels like to build something alone. It was never meant to be more than that. But it kept growing, and at some point it became something worth sharing. So here it is. Hope it's useful.

I've spent my career at the intersection of games, 3D and brand. If you need someone for brand design, creative direction or consulting — and Orby gives you some confidence in what I do — I'd love to hear from you. Get in touch.
