3D assets — overview

Orby (the software, brand, UI, and original art we created) is separate from the third-party 3D models used in marketing screenshots, turntable frames, and optional demo files. Creators and platforms below retain ownership of those models; we use them under the licenses shown on each source page.

  • Your work in the app: models you load stay yours. Orby does not claim ownership of your files; see the Privacy Policy.
  • Marketing on orby.studio: stills and video are renders made in Orby from downloaded meshes—we distribute images and encoded frame sequences, not the raw third-party .glb files unless noted below.
  • Trademarks: product and vehicle names (for example Audi, Jeep, New Balance) belong to their respective owners; use here is for identification in credits only. If you represent a brand and do not want your intellectual property shown in Orby marketing, please get in touch—we will review your request and remove or replace the relevant material promptly.

Inline lower-thirds on the homepage mirror this section where possible. If a source link is missing, check the creator’s Sketchfab profile or contact orby-admin@proton.me and we will add it.

Orby is non-commercial. orby.studio and the Orby app are a free personal studio project—no paid product, no ads, no sale of third-party meshes. Marketing on this site is there to show what the viewer can do, not to monetize the artwork itself.

For our use case—on-site renders only, no redistribution of raw .glb files in the public repo—that lines up with how these licenses are meant to work. Where we rely on permissive community licenses (for example Meshy Community CC0), we are comfortable that our marketing use is covered; we still credit creators out of courtesy.

The Lemur and Prismatic Dragon assets were downloaded from the Meshy Community gallery (published under CC0 per Meshy’s Terms of Use and Meshy Help Center). CC0 allows commercial use without requiring attribution; we credit @cyber_fox and kaesar3d anyway. Orby’s operator also has a paid Meshy plan for models they generate themselves—that ownership rule applies to their own outputs, not to these community downloads.
