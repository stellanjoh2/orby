3D assets — overview

Orby (the software, brand, UI, and original art) is separate from the third-party 3D models used in marketing screenshots, turntable frames, and optional demo files. Creators and platforms below retain ownership of those models; Orby uses them under the licenses shown on each source page.

  • Files opened in the app: models loaded in the browser stay with the person who opened them. Orby does not claim ownership of those files; see the Privacy Policy.
  • Marketing on orby.studio: stills and video are renders made in Orby from downloaded meshes. This site publishes images and encoded frame sequences, not the raw third-party .glb files.
  • Trademarks: product and vehicle names (for example Audi, Jeep, New Balance) belong to their respective owners; use here is for identification in credits only. Brand owners who do not want their intellectual property shown in Orby marketing can contact Orby; requests are reviewed and relevant material is removed or replaced when appropriate.

Inline lower-thirds on large homepage feature sections (not the small pro feature cards) mirror this section where possible. Missing source links can be reported to orby-admin@proton.me.

Orby is non-commercial. orby.studio and the Orby app are a free personal studio project—no paid product, no ads, no sale of third-party meshes. Marketing on this site demonstrates what the viewer can do in the app, not to monetize the underlying artwork.

Orby’s use is limited to on-site renders—not redistribution of raw .glb files in the public repository. That fits licenses such as Meshy Community CC0 and other permissive terms listed below. Creators are credited on this page and in the UI as a courtesy, even when attribution is not required.
