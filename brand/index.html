Logo

The Orby logotype is the primary mark. Default to lime on Orby black; white on dark, or black on white or lime, work when you need a reversed treatment. See Allowed combinations for other approved pairings.

Do not edit, distort, or reconfigure the logo. Recolor only with approved palette pairings (see Allowed combinations). A full brand kit with SVG and PNG exports will be available here soon.

Symbol

The orb mark works when the full logotype is already visible nearby — app icons, favicons, and compact UI chrome. Same color rules as the logotype.

Colors

Orby Lime and Orby Black are the primary colors — lime for the logotype and primary actions, black for backgrounds and type on bright surfaces. Orby Purple, Orby Blue, Orby Pink, and Orby Surface muted are supporting colors: purple for UI and gradients, blue for inline links, pink for warnings and severity, surface muted for panels and chrome.

  • Orby Lime #C4FF00
  • Orby Purple #3B00FF
  • Orby Blue #00C4FF
  • Orby Pink #FF00C4
  • Orby Black #080808
  • Orby Surface muted #202020

Allowed combinations

Lime on black is the default — use it wherever you can. These are the approved solid pairings. Orby Lime and white logotypes also work on top of renders when you need a branded overlay. Do not invent new colors or add effects to the logo.

Usage

Do

  • Use Orby marks to show you use Orby or link to the app.
  • Keep logos and colors as provided — no gradients or effects on the mark itself.
  • Use “Orby” as an adjective (“Orby viewer”, “built with Orby”).
  • Link to orby.studio when directing people to the product.

Don’t

  • Imply Orby sponsors, endorses, or employs you without permission.
  • Put Orby marks in your product name, logo, domain, or social handle.
  • Modify, rotate, outline, or combine the mark with other logos.
  • Use Orby assets on merchandise without written approval.
