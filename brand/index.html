Logo

The Orby logotype is the primary mark. Use lime or white on dark backgrounds, or black on white or lime when you need a reversed treatment.

Do not edit, distort, recolor, or reconfigure the logo. A full brand kit with SVG and PNG exports will be available here soon.

Symbol

The orb mark works when the full logotype is already visible nearby — app icons, favicons, and compact UI chrome. Same color rules as the logotype.

Colors

Lime and purple are the signature pair — lime for the logotype and primary actions; purple for UI, links, and gradients. Orby black anchors backgrounds.

  • Lime #C4FF00
  • Purple #7E6BFF
  • Orby black #080808
  • Surface muted #151515

Usage

Do

  • Use Orby marks to show you use Orby or link to the app.
  • Keep logos and colors as provided — no gradients or effects on the mark itself.
  • Use “Orby” as an adjective (“Orby viewer”, “built with Orby”).
  • Link to orby.studio when directing people to the product.

Don’t

  • Imply Orby sponsors, endorses, or employs you without permission.
  • Put Orby marks in your product name, logo, domain, or social handle.
  • Modify, rotate, outline, or combine the mark with other logos.
  • Use Orby assets on merchandise without written approval.
